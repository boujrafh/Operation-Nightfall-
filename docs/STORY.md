# Operation Nightfall — Story Bible

## Premise

**1997. The Kessrin Plateau.** A rogue private army, **IRON VEIL**, has taken over an old weather
station. Beneath it they are building **GOLIATH-7**, a walking missile platform that can fire from
anywhere.

You are **Agent WREN**, a rookie in the covert unit **SPECTRE-9**. Your veteran partner
**KESTREL** went in three days ago and went silent. You go in alone, with nothing but a codec
radio. **Commander HALE** guides you from HQ — until his advice starts leading you into traps.

## Cast

- **Wren** — the player. Quiet, determined, dry humour.
- **Hale** — calm father figure on the radio; the founder of IRON VEIL. His portrait glitches from Chapter 5.
- **Kestrel** — sarcastic veteran, loyal to the end.
- **Lark** — resistance fighter, voice only until the ending. "Trust your eyes, not every voice on the radio."
- **Bix** — talkative quartermaster; explains every gadget.
- **Dr. Sato** — medic; saves your progress.
- **Dr. Ilse Varga** — engineer forced to build GOLIATH-7; **Mira**, her daughter, held hostage.

## Bosses

Brask (riot shield, charges) · Echo (optical camouflage) · Twin Hounds (linked lives) · Cinder
(fire spreading on tiles) · Puppeteer (mind control, hostage shields) · Static (EMP) · GOLIATH-7
(walking missile platform) · Hale "Overwatch" (predicts your moves, bullet time).

## Inspiration

The structure follows the MSX2 infiltration classics of 1987: a lone agent, a fortified base, key
cards, rescued prisoners, a radio that turns out to be lying, a war machine in the basement and an
escape against the clock. Names, characters and dialogue are original.
