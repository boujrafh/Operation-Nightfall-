# 01 · The Golden Circle

**Status:** Approved

## WHY: the core belief

The best stealth games make you feel **clever, not powerful**. Players today rarely get the
tension of 8-bit infiltration games: one guard, one vision cone, one heartbeat. We believe that
tension should be **available to anyone, anywhere, instantly**, with no install, store or download.

## HOW: what makes it different

1. **Learn by doing, step by step.** Each chapter adds exactly one new mechanic. Weapons are
   earned through the story, not handed out at the start.
2. **Real MSX2 look and feel:** a limited 16-colour palette, room-by-room screen transitions and
   chiptune audio made live in the browser (Web Audio, no audio files).
3. **Readable stealth:** vision cones are always visible, and alert states are clear. When you
   fail, you can see exactly why.
4. **Plays anywhere, offline:** a single-file game packaged as an installable PWA. It works with
   keyboard or touch, and your progress is saved.
5. **A codec story with a twist:** the radio is your guide, until it starts lying to you.
6. **A cast of distinct characters and bosses with powers.** Each boss works as a puzzle built
   around one special ability, with its own music and sounds that set the mood.

## WHAT: the product

**OPERATION NIGHTFALL** is a top-down 8-bit stealth action game playable in the browser,
delivered as an offline-capable PWA. You play Agent Wren infiltrating the IRON VEIL base across
connected rooms. You avoid patrols and cameras, collect key cards and gear, rescue allies, uncover
your commander's betrayal, destroy the GOLIATH-7 war machine and escape.

## Inspiration and legal note

The game is inspired by the structure of the 1987 MSX2 classic of the infiltration genre. All
names, characters, story and art in this project are original. Only the genre and its mechanics
(vision cones, alert phases, room-based maps, key cards, radio support) are borrowed.
