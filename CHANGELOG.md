# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Chapter 0 "Insertion" (unreleased, awaiting playtest)

- 3 connected rooms: Beach, Fence Line, Outer Yard.
- Guards with patrol routes, visible vision cones and line of sight.
- INFILTRATION / ALERT / EVASION states, reinforcements, guards following you between rooms.
- Hiding in bushes, behind crates and in the cardboard box; noisy metal grating.
- Punches and takedowns from behind; Key Card 1 dropped by a guard; level 1 door.
- Codec calls with portraits (Hale, Bix, Lark, Dr. Sato); save through Dr. Sato and auto-save.
- MSX-style procedural music (title, infiltration, alert, evasion, codec, game over, victory) and sound effects.
- Title, briefing, pause, options, game over and chapter results with a rank.
- PWA: installable, fully offline, pixel-art icons, update prompt.
- Touch controls (stick + A/B/W): side layout in landscape, handheld-console layout in portrait;
  the stick also navigates menus; TOUCH PAD option (AUTO / ON / OFF).
- Saves with checksum, safe write, quarantine of corrupted data and memory fallback.
